🔬 Multi-Task Spatiotemporal AI Model Architecture

End-to-end early warning workflow: Multimodal atmospheric inputs → Shared feature backbone → 3 Prediction Heads → Terrain Fusion

Multi-Task Deep Learning Framework
Stage 1: Ingestion
Multimodal Observational Data
Continuous ingestion of INSAT-3D/3DR satellite channels, IMDAA atmospheric thermodynamics, GPM precipitation rasters, and SRTM 30m DEM.
INSAT-3D WV IMDAA TMP/WIND GPM IMERG SRTM 30m
Stage 2: Features
Atmospheric Predictors Backbone
Extraction of physical catalysts: Column Moisture (IWV), Thermodynamic Instability (CAPE/CIN), Kinematic Lift (Convergence & Shear), and Satellite Updraft (CTT Drop Rate).
IWV > 50mm CAPE > 2500 J/kg CTT Rate -18°C/h Conv > 3.5×10⁻⁵
Stage 3: Inference
3 Multi-Task Prediction Heads
A shared ConvLSTM/CNN backbone feeds three specialized dense prediction heads outputting calibrated probabilities (0–100%) across the nowcast window.
⚡ Thunderstorm Head 🌧️ Cloudburst Head 🌊 Flash Flood Head
Stage 4: Fusion
Terrain-Aware Risk Engine
Predicted hazard probabilities are fused with SRTM 30m topographic relief, slope steepness, and catchment drainage bottlenecks to evaluate hyper-local vulnerability.
SRTM Slope > 35° Basin Bottleneck Composite Risk %

2–6h Nowcast & Explainable AI

Dataset Provenance & Technical Architecture

This prototype is powered by the scientific datasets located inside the current project workspace and integrates multimodal remote sensing:

1. SRTM 30m Digital Elevation Model

Role: High-Resolution Elevation & Terrain Catalyst
Source: Data/SRTM_30m_South_India/.../SRTM_30m_South_India.tif
Dimensions: 37,260 × 29,686 (1.1 Billion 30-meter elevation cells)
Usage: Shaded relief terrain layer, slope estimation, elevation query grid, and Western Ghats topographic cross-section transect.

2. NASA GPM IMERG Daily GeoTIFFs

Role: Satellite Precipitation Estimation
Source: Data/GPM/GPM_IMERG_2024_Jan_Day_1..30.tif
Dimensions: 30 daily GeoTIFFs (368 × 335 at 0.0898° resolution)
Usage: 30-day daily precipitation maps, peak event detection (up to 277.8 mm/day), timeline playback scrubber, and rainfall time series.

3. IMDAA Atmospheric Reanalysis

Role: Thermodynamic & Kinematic Baseline
Source: Data/Temperature/IMDAA_TMP_2m...nc, IMDAA_APCP...nc, Data/wind/IMDAA_UGRD/VGRD...nc
Dimensions: 48,926 timesteps × 32 × 32 grid (1.08° resolution)
Usage: 2m Air Temp (°C), Surface APCP, 10m U/V Winds, kinematic convergence, and convective instability baselines.

4. INSAT-3D/3DR Satellite Observations

Role: Cloud Updraft Signature & Water Vapor Fuel
Channels: 10.8µm Thermal IR (Cloud Top Temperature - CTT), 6.7µm Water Vapor (Integrated Water Vapor - IWV)
Usage: Detects explosive convective updrafts via rapid CTT cooling rates (< -15°C/hr) and deep tropospheric moisture fuel.

5. BharathBench AI Deep Learning Models

Role: AI Spatiotemporal Weather Benchmarks
Source: Data/BharathBench/Table4Models/*.hdf5
Architectures: CNN & ConvLSTM neural networks (Val Loss: 0.0266 for T2m, 0.1473 for H500)
Usage: Scientific foundation for multi-task spatiotemporal forecasting heads.

Authenticity Guarantee: All maps, elevation readings, rainfall charts, and climate baselines are processed directly from the raw TIFF and NetCDF files without altering original datasets.
Nowcasting Disclaimer: 2–6 hour future nowcasting states represent a prototype inference engine calibrated on physical predictors for hackathon demonstration.
